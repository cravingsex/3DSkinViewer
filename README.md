**SkinViewer** brings your Minecraft character to life directly inside your menus.

Enjoy a clean and interactive **3D preview of your current Minecraft skin** on the title screen or in the pause menu. Rotate your character, customize the viewer's position, and give your menus a little more personality. 🎮


## 🌟 Features

### 🧍 Interactive 3D Skin Viewer

Take a closer look at your character whenever you want.

* View your player model directly in the **Title Screen** and **Pause Menu**
* Uses Minecraft's native player rendering for an authentic in-game appearance

---

### 🎨 Your Current Minecraft Skin

* Automatically displays the currently loaded skin of your local player
* Supports both **Steve** and **Alex** player models
* Renders enabled skin layers, including:

  * 🎩 Hat layer
  * 🧥 Jacket layer
  * 👕 Sleeves
  * 👖 Pants layers
  * 🦸 Cape, when available

---

### 🏷️ Menu Name Display

Make your menus feel a little more personal.

* Optionally display your Minecraft username above the SkinViewer
* Available in both the **Title Screen** and **Pause Menu**

---

### 👤 Third-Person NameTag

See your name tag while viewing your character in third person.

* Optional third-person name tag for your own player
* Automatically hidden while playing in first person for a clean experience

---

### ⚙️ Simple Customization

Make SkinViewer fit your style.

* Choose whether the viewer appears on the **Left** or **Right** side
* Toggle the menu name independently from the third-person name tag
* All settings are saved automatically ✨

---

### 🧩 Compatibility

Simple, lightweight, and easy to use.

* **Client-side only** — no server installation required
* Works perfectly **without Mod Menu**
* Optional **Mod Menu configuration support**
* Designed for **Fabric on Minecraft `26.2`**

---

## 🖱️ Controls

| Action                   | Control                   |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| 🔄 Rotate the SkinViewer | **Left-click + Drag**     |
| ⚙️ Open Settings         | **Mod Menu → SkinViewer** |

---

## 📦 Installation

Getting started is quick and easy:

1. Install **[Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/installer/)** for Minecraft `26.2`
2. Install **[Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)**
3. Place `skinviewer-1.0.0.jar` into your `.minecraft/mods` folder
4. Launch Minecraft using your **Fabric profile**

---
